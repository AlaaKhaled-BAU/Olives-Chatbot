5.0 Routes
One of the most obvious benefits of a Sales Automation System is what is called “Routes”, they are routes that a
salesman is obliged to follow which contains his assigned customers locations. by following this route for each sales-
man, sales process becomes more efficient as it saves time, reduces vehicle and fuel consumption, effort, and avoid
overlap of visits of customers by different salesmen and allows accurate tracking of a salesman travel.

5.8 Routes - Import Route Info from Excel
In many occasions , you may need to Upload a number of routes with all related data such as position routes custom-
er route assignments ..etc. simultaneously, you can do this through the previously routes existed windows, but the
process might be time consuming, therefore , this window was developed for this purpose to help user to upload the
routes in one shot.
Using this left window you first download the
Excel template which will contain all fields need-
ed to be filled to establish a full ready for work
route for salesman by pressing the Download
Template Excel File, you fill this excel with all
required data , and using the Browse button you
upload the filled excel template to Olives System,
if the step succeeds without any errors shown,
you would have finished routes preparation.
