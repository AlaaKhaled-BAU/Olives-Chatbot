1.7 Upload Order
This is the second icon a sales man will find in the main external menu just after he logs with his ID and password,
it is the item count of his van.

Tapping the icon of Upload order will open window in image 44, you will see items categories on the left once you tap a category sub
category/categories will appear and you can choose one by tapping the desired category which will result showing the items of that
chosen category on the right once you tap an item, Image 44-B pop up will appear to set the item quantity, but you should choose
store from the blue icon in image 44 which will open the image 44-C pop up window then you can save the upload order using save
desk icon.
Having chosen all desired item quantities they will appear under Items in
the black sprite in the middle left of window in Image 44-D, assuming
that you have also concluded choosing Store as in previous step, saving
the upload order by tapping the desk save icon will show the print dia-
logue pop up window in Image 44-E below, so you can either print tap-
ping the printer icon or go back two steps to the main upload screen.
