# 1.12 Settings-Positions
This window was developed in the system to link a number of privileges (that we call a basket of Privileges) to
certain salesman/salesmen, this feature enables flexibility in linking sales persons to these privileges or depriving
them of those privileges (like in case of transition of a salesperson from one division to another) or ( canceling a
person and assigning another to the same position in case of resigning ), so you do not have to set privileges each
time for the same position, you just need to change the salesman name.
Opening a position record will show this
window, required data are entered here
Image 15 (Positions Window)
Note: Positions are one of the most important fundamental elements of the system because a salesman assigned to
a position performs the core action of a Business which is a transaction such as a Sales Invoice, a Payment, Or-
der ...etc.
