12 Dashboards
Dashboards Main Tab contains several sub Dashboard tabs that consist of very useful analysis tools that will give
you a general idea about sales and salesman average performance in a certain period regrading different types of
transactions, we will be discussing some examples of those dashboards
# 12.1 Dashboard-Dashboard Sales Analysis
This Dashboard shows general analysis of sales processed that have been performed by salesmen in a single date
of your choice , after selecting the date click on the refresh “Two Orange Arrows “button which will show top
most significant salesmen results in terms of : Route Score by salesman , Salesman General daily visits Score,
Salesman Visits Verification , Salesman Journey Performance , & Salesman Route Sales Comparison.

# 12.2 Dashboard-Product Performance
This Dashboard is very useful for you to be able to see your product performance in the market with regard to
their sales process results you first need to select your desired filters and then click the refresh “Two Orange Ar-
rows “button, which will show you the results in a cake pie chart with illustration on each section.
Product Performance
# 12.3 Dashboard-Sales Growth
This Dashboard is useful to compare your total sales during a certain period with that of the same period but for
last year you first need to select your desired filters and then click the refresh “Two Orange Arrows “button, any
minus figure rate will show a decrease in total sales comparing with the same period of last year and every posi-
tive figure will show an increase in total sales comparing with the same period of last year and if gauge is on 0 this
means you have the same total sales.

# 12.4 Dashboard-Salesman KPIs
This dashboards is a bout KPIs of salesman which are (key performance indicators) for each salesman ,having se-
lected the salesman month , and year from the existed filters , you can then press “Preview” to see your results
on gauge forms which also shows percentages, these percentages are calculated based on comparisons, for ex-
ample the first gauge will compare the sales amount to sales target (if exists) , for the selected salesperson, you
can always press the left or right small black arrows which will lead you to the previous or next salesman and up-
date the data without having to press Preview again, note that the upper value is always compared to the lower
as in example.
