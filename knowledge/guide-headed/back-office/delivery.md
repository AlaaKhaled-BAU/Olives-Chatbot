13 Delivery
This relatively new main tab was added to the system to allow the user to assign a driver to deliver goods that are
already purchased by a customer , at the same time monitoring the delivery process in terms of its completion .
# 13.1 Delivery-Invoice Delivery
Using this tab you can see the invoices with delivery tasks and their status in terms of assigned delivery man , and
being delivered or not , you first need to filter your results according to your desire .
Invoice Delivery
# 13.2 Delivery-Delivery Cars
This tab is used to set basic information about the delivery cars inside the details if you happen to click the add or
the edit icons you would see details such as name ,Load Weight .

This tab is used to assign a certain invoice goods delivery to a certain salesman , after filtering , you choose the
Delivery man and press the “Approve” button after right checking the box beside the invoices .
