# 1.12 Alerts
This Icon once opened will show you alerts sent by the back office system to the sales man it will mainly consist of
work flow approvals or denials to certain requests which was sent by the sales man to his supervisor.
Icon

have already dealt with, you can tap the litter icon existed on
