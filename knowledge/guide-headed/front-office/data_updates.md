# 1.11 Data Updates
This is the tool that by using a sales man can update all his data , this data will include everything sent by back
office such as customers details , items details , price lists ...etc. at the same time, it will transfer all his transac-
tions to the back office system and consequently to ERP system.
Once you tap “Data Updates” icon -as seen in Image 49-A–
Icon you will have three options of data update : Update Images,
Upload Only, and Upload Action Log . A sales man is generally
asked to make update to images and action log which will
include everything (Back office data, and sending his transac-
tions) , after making the right choices he taps the Update
Data.
Image 49-A (Data Updates main window )
Image 49-B (Update Data Progress window) Image 49-C (Update Data completion window)
Image 49-B & Image 49-C demonstrate “update in progress”
procedure and “update finish” state , you can have two results
either the left window will not complete to 100% and cut in
some points and then the list on the right image will have red
crosses beside some headers, or it will complete to 100%
which means that the update was completed successfully you
will have right green checks beside each header , most im-
portant right checks should be on vital issues such as im-
porting customers salesman , items price list ...etc. Simultane-
ously, transactions must be transferred successfully to the
server.
