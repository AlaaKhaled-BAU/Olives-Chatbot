# 1.11 Collect Customers GPS Locations
One of the privileges of a sales automation system is recording GPS locations of company’s customers, this infor-
mation is very useful for more than a reason such as : preserving the customer location in the system , sales analy-
sis in relation to areas , setting a route for a certain sales man, forcing a salesman to log into his customer using
GPS location ...etc.
Icon
By tapping the “Collecting Customer GPS Locations” tab this
window will appear, a salesman can choose a customer by
tapping Route or customers and selecting the category by
tapping it and then tapping the customer which will show the
list of customers from which he select his desired customer, he
can also search for a customer using the magnifier search tool
icon located on the tool bar in the upper right corner of the
window (see Image 48-A).
