# 1.10 Add Customer
This tool is made to enable a salesperson to add a new customer , but this is a preliminary step before ERP & Back
office administrators would accept this customer and add him as a system registered customer having all neces-
sary options as relating to certain group and type with certain financial details and prepare him for interacting
with a salesman .
Icon
By tapping the “Add Customer” icon this window will appear a
sales man should enter all required data and then tap the save
desk icon located on the top right corner in the tools bar, he
can also add a photo through his tablet through the Camera
icon which by tapping will open the tablet Camera.
