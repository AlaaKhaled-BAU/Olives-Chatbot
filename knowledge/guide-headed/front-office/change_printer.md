As you see from the left Image no. 57 when you tap the
Current Printer address change printer address icon this window will open , you
will have the linked Bluetooth address in the top area of
Other Printers’ Available addresses the left image (if 0=no printer address) and all other
available printers below the blue thin line , you should
note however that they should be paired from blue tooth
in tablet settings before they are listed here, once you
choose the printer it will appear up the line (current
selected printer) after that you need to tap Save at the
bottom area of the window to save address.
